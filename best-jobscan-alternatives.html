Best Jobscan alternatives in 2026.

Jobscan has been the default ATS resume scanner since 2013, but its cheapest paid plan is $49.95/month with no per-resume option, and it returns a score and suggestions rather than a finished resume. If you only tailor a few resumes a month, that is a lot to pay. Here are the best alternatives, ranked honestly on price, output, and whether you have to create an account.

The short answer

If you want the cheapest option with no subscription and no account, ATSHack ($1 per tailored resume) is the pick. If you want the most generous free tier, Teal wins. If you want a free match score like Jobscan's, Resume Worded and ATSHack's free grader both do that. If you want a cheaper unlimited subscription, SkillSyncer at $14.95/month is the closest like-for-like.

1. ATSHack — cheapest, no account, rewrites the resume

ATSHack is the only tool here that charges per resume instead of per month. You upload your resume, paste the job posting, and for $1 it returns a finished, ATS-optimized PDF and editable DOCX with your existing experience rewritten to match the posting's keywords. No account, no email, no subscription on the $1 path. There is also a free 0–100 grader if you just want a score first, and an optional Pro plan ($15/month or $99/year) for 50 tailorings, cover letters, ATS gap reports, and a STAR interview coach. Best for job seekers who apply to a handful of roles and want a finished file, not homework.

2. Teal — best free tier

Teal bundles a resume builder, a Chrome extension, and a job application tracker into a genuinely useful free plan. Its AI features and unlimited use sit behind Teal+ (around $9/week or $29/month). If you want one dashboard to manage your whole search and you do not mind creating an account, Teal's free tier is the most generous on this list.

3. Resume Worded — best free score

Resume Worded's "Score My Resume" and LinkedIn review tools give you a detailed, line-by-line breakdown for free, which is the closest thing to Jobscan's score without paying. Its deeper features are subscription-gated and users note the paid tier is not cheap. Best for diagnosing what is weak before you fix it.

4. SkillSyncer — cheapest subscription

SkillSyncer is the most direct "cheaper Jobscan" — same keyword-match-score model at $14.95/month instead of $49.95. Free plan is one scan per week, and it is free for students and military. Best if you specifically want unlimited match scans and prefer a subscription to pay-per-use.

5. Rezi — full AI builder

Rezi is an AI resume builder with ATS optimization baked in, at $29/month or a one-time $149 lifetime license. It is more than a scanner — it builds the whole resume. Best if you are starting from scratch rather than tailoring an existing resume.

How to choose

  • You only apply to a few jobs a month: ATSHack ($1 each, no account).
  • You want one dashboard for the whole search: Teal (free tier).
  • You want to diagnose a weak resume for free: Resume Worded or ATSHack's grader.
  • You apply constantly and want unlimited scans cheap: SkillSyncer ($14.95/month).
  • You are building a resume from scratch: Rezi.
