Templates vs tailoring

Kickresume's value is its templates and its library of resumes from people hired at well-known companies, great for inspiration and for building from scratch. But a template does not tailor itself to each posting. ATSHack takes whatever resume you have and rewrites it to match the specific job, which is the step templates do not cover.

Pricing: per-resume vs subscription

Kickresume runs about $19/month, less billed annually, and is free for students. ATSHack is $1 per tailored resume, no subscription on the no-account path. For a short search you pay only for the resumes you actually tailor.

ATS-safe output

Some template builders use layouts that look polished but parse poorly in an ATS. ATSHack outputs single-column, standard-header formatting designed to pass the software screen first.
