# ATSHack vNext — resume / ATS domain excellence

This artifact defines the quality bar that concept pages must demonstrate before the design is allowed to sell ATSHack as a premium resume product.

## Core principle

ATSHack is not a keyword-stuffing or score-chasing product. The valuable transformation is:

**source evidence → target requirements → truthful rewrite → parser-safe document → human-readable proof of competence**

The website is weak if the visual design is excellent but the resume transformation itself is mediocre.

## Truth boundary

The tailored resume may:
- preserve and reorder true experience;
- make vague source language more specific when the source packet supports the specificity;
- mirror target-job terminology when the candidate has evidence for it;
- surface supported tools, skills, certifications, scope, and outcomes;
- improve hierarchy, clarity, density, and ATS-safe structure.

The tailored resume must not:
- invent years of experience;
- invent employers, titles, credentials, tools, certifications, awards, metrics, team sizes, budgets, volumes, or outcomes;
- insert a target keyword just because it appears in the job description;
- convert an unsupported preference into claimed experience;
- imply an interview, ranking, ATS passage, or hiring outcome that ATSHack cannot prove.

An unsupported requirement is a **gap**, not a writing prompt.

## What excellent output looks like

A strong ATSHack result should pass both a machine and a recruiter reading test.

### Target alignment
- The target role is explicit.
- Important job-description language is mapped to actual source evidence.
- High-value supported keywords appear naturally in summary, skills, and experience.
- Unsupported requirements remain visibly excluded.

### Summary quality
The summary should identify the candidate's real function, strongest relevant operating context, and supported target-role competencies. It should avoid generic traits such as "hardworking," "results-driven," or "team player" unless the surrounding sentence proves something concrete.

### Bullet quality
A strong bullet usually contains:
1. a clear action;
2. the business/technical object of the action;
3. useful scope or operating context when the source supports it;
4. a result or metric only when the source supports it;
5. target-role terminology only where it remains truthful.

The product should never add a number merely to make a bullet look stronger.

### ATS-safe structure
- ordinary section names;
- text-based content;
- conventional reading order;
- no decorative layout that hides critical text from parsers;
- clean PDF plus editable document output when that capability is available;
- human legibility remains the primary visual goal.

## Canonical illustrative source packet

The three Round 3 concepts should use the same source packet so the visual directions are judged on the same business truth.

### Target role
**Operations Specialist**

Target posting emphasizes:
- inventory reconciliation;
- Excel;
- KPI reporting;
- cross-functional coordination;
- process improvement;
- SOP ownership (preferred).

### Candidate
**Jordan Lee — Operations Coordinator, Northstar Logistics — Dallas, TX**

### Raw source facts supplied by the candidate
- Performs daily inventory counts across receiving, storage, and outbound operations.
- Investigates inventory discrepancies in Excel and maintains the cycle-count log.
- Builds the weekly KPI report for warehouse managers covering inventory accuracy, order exceptions, and on-time departures.
- Coordinates warehouse and transportation teams when inventory or dispatch exceptions block customer orders.
- Updated the cycle-count checklist after recurring discrepancies by adding a second verification step.
- No evidence supplied for owning formal SOP documentation.

### Weak resume version
**Summary**
> Hardworking operations professional looking for a new opportunity.

**Experience**
- Helped with inventory.
- Made reports for managers.
- Worked with different departments.
- Helped improve the inventory process.

**Skills**
Excel · Inventory · Reporting · Teamwork

### Gold-standard tailored version
**Summary**
> Operations Coordinator experienced in inventory reconciliation, Excel-based KPI reporting, and cross-functional issue resolution across receiving, storage, and outbound workflows.

**Experience**
- Reconciled daily inventory counts across receiving, storage, and outbound operations, investigating discrepancies in Excel and maintaining cycle-count records.
- Built weekly KPI reports on inventory accuracy, order exceptions, and on-time departures for warehouse managers.
- Coordinated warehouse and transportation teams to resolve inventory and dispatch exceptions blocking customer orders.
- Updated the cycle-count checklist after recurring discrepancies, adding a second verification step to the counting workflow.

**Core skills**
Inventory reconciliation · Excel · KPI reporting · Cross-functional coordination · Process improvement

**Explicit gap**
SOP ownership is requested by the target posting but is not supported by the source packet, so ATSHack must not add it.

## Why the after version is materially better

It does not merely swap verbs. It converts vague responsibilities into evidence-backed operating language, makes the candidate's relevant domain visible, mirrors supported target-role terminology, preserves a clean ATS-safe structure, and gives a recruiter concrete reasons to understand the candidate's fit.

The transformation should visually show **why each stronger phrase is allowed**. The product story is more credible when it proves that one tempting keyword was intentionally left out.

## Website design implications

Every concept should make at least some of the following visible:
- target requirement → source evidence mapping;
- supported vs unsupported keyword state;
- weak line → strong source-backed rewrite;
- job-specific ordering/emphasis;
- document hierarchy and parser-safe structure;
- exact resume version tied to an application in Pro;
- no fake hireability score or guaranteed outcome.

The canonical transformation is illustrative and must be labeled as such. It is acceptable to use a fictional candidate for a concept study because the page is demonstrating product mechanics, not claiming a real customer outcome.

## Research basis used for Round 3

Round 3 was informed by current 2026 guidance from Teal and Jobscan emphasizing job-specific tailoring, using target-job terminology where truthful, addressing gaps, prioritizing relevant experience, strong action/impact bullets, measurable accomplishments only when supported, and avoiding keyword stuffing. The concept deliberately treats these as writing/product principles rather than as guaranteed hiring outcomes.
