Signature mechanic

An edit you can watch happen.

The page itself becomes the product story: identify weak language, map it to the target role, trace every stronger phrase back to source evidence, leave unsupported requirements out, then export the cleaner document.

01 / SOURCE

Keep the history.

The baseline resume stays visible. ATSHack is not allowed to invent employers, credentials, achievements, skills, metrics, or years of experience.

02 / MAP

Separate evidence from gaps.

Inventory reconciliation, Excel, KPI reporting, cross-functional coordination, and process improvement are supported by the source packet. SOP ownership is not, so it stays out.

03 / REWRITE

Replace vague with specific.

Weak task wording becomes concrete operating language: what Jordan did, where it happened, what artifact was produced, and which target-role terms are genuinely supported.

04 / EXPORT

Hand back a document, not a score.

The end product is a clean resume file with stronger evidence density and clearer role alignment. No fake interview probability attached.
