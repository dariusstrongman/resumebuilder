Why teacher applications get screened out

01Certifications buried or stripped. State license, endorsements, and grade-level authorizations need to be visible and worded the way the posting asks. Civilian tools treat them as clutter.
02Wrong keywords for the subject and grade band. A posting for “secondary ELA” will not match a resume that only says “high school English” unless the wording is aligned.
03Multi-column or designed templates. District systems like Frontline (Applitrack) and SearchSoft parse single-column plain text best; fancy layouts garble.
04Methodology and curriculum terms missing. Differentiated instruction, IEP/504, PBIS, MTSS, and the specific curriculum named in the posting need to appear if they are part of your real experience.
05Student-outcome results not surfaced. Growth scores, proficiency gains, and program results make you credible but get cut to fit a one-page template.
