The export paywall

Zety's build-free-then-pay-to-download model is the single most common complaint about it online. You invest time building a resume, then discover you cannot download it without paying, and the cheap-looking trial often auto-renews into a monthly charge. ATSHack states the price up front: $1 for the tailored resume, paid once, no account, no auto-renewal on that path.

Pricing transparency

Zety's effective price is around $24.70/month after the trial. ATSHack is a flat $1 per resume. There is an optional $15/month Pro tier if you want a saved dashboard, but it is clearly optional. The core product is the $1 one-time tailor.

Build vs tailor

Zety is a from-scratch builder with templates and examples. ATSHack tailors a resume you already have to a specific posting. If you have no resume at all, a builder makes sense; just watch the download paywall. If you have a resume, ATSHack tailors it for a dollar.
