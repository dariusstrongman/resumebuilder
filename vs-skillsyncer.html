ATSHack vs SkillSyncer.

SkillSyncer is one of the most popular cheaper-than-Jobscan scanners, around $14.95/month with one free scan a week, and free for students and military. It tells you how well your resume matches a posting. ATSHack goes a step further: instead of scoring the gap, it rewrites your resume to close it and hands you a finished PDF and DOCX for $1, no subscription.

Score vs finished resume

SkillSyncer is a scanner: it shows your match percentage and the keywords you are missing, then you go rewrite the resume yourself. ATSHack includes a free 0–100 grader that does the same scoring, but the paid step actually rewrites the resume for you and returns the file. You skip the manual edit.

Pricing: $1 once vs $14.95/month

SkillSyncer is already much cheaper than Jobscan's $49.95/month, which is its main selling point. But it is still a subscription. If you only need a few resumes, $1 each on ATSHack is cheaper than a single month of SkillSyncer. SkillSyncer's free-for-students-and-military offer is genuinely good if you qualify.

Where SkillSyncer wins

If you want unlimited keyword scans and you like working in a scanner interface to learn what is missing, SkillSyncer at $14.95/month is a fair deal, especially free for students. ATSHack is built for people who would rather have the rewrite done than do it themselves.
