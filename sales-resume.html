Why sales applications get screened out

01Quota attainment buried. “Exceeded quota” means nothing without the number. 142% of a $2M quota belongs at the top of the bullet, not hidden in prose.
02CRM keywords missing. Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, and the tools the posting names get scanned for by name; vague “CRM experience” can miss.
03Methodology not mentioned. MEDDIC, Challenger, Sandler, SPIN, and the segment (SMB, mid-market, enterprise) need to match the posting when they match your experience.
04Deal metrics not surfaced. Average deal size, sales cycle length, pipeline generated, and ramp time are credibility signals that get cut for space.
05Generic titles. “Sales rep” vs the posting's “Account Executive” or “Business Development Representative” matters to the keyword screen.

What ATSHack formats correctly for sales

01Quota attainment, deal size, and pipeline numbers pulled to the front of your bullets, using only the figures already on your resume.
02CRM tools and methodology keywords from the posting mirrored where they truthfully apply (Salesforce, HubSpot, MEDDIC, Challenger, and the named segment).
03Title alignment so your experience matches the exact role the posting names, without misrepresenting seniority.
04Single-column, standard-header output that Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and other systems parse cleanly.
05Refusal to fabricate. We will not invent quota numbers, deal sizes, or President's Club awards that are not on your source resume.
